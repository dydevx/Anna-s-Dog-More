# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Design-conscious dog owners in Switzerland and nearby European markets who want durable, comfortable products that belong in a considered home. They may browse in German or English, often on mobile, and need confidence in exact variants, availability, delivery, returns, and payment before buying.

Store staff use the administration area to maintain products, variant stock, orders, and shipping configuration without touching code.

## Product Purpose

Anna's Dog & More is a bilingual commerce storefront for a curated LABONI assortment. It turns verified LABONI catalog data into an accessible purchase flow with relational product variants, persistent cart, server-validated checkout, payment-provider abstraction, inventory control, customer accounts, and an authenticated administration surface.

Success means a customer can discover a product, understand the exact configuration, add the correct SKU, receive an accurate server-calculated total, and complete a real provider-hosted payment. Staff must be able to manage the resulting order without exposing privileged credentials or customer data.

## Brand Personality

Quietly assured, tactile, and exact. The experience should feel like a well-composed Zurich interior shop: warm natural light, carefully chosen objects, generous space, and information that never becomes precious or vague.

## Anti-references

- Discount pet-supermarket visuals, novelty paw-print decoration, bright category colors, and playful bubble typography.
- Generic beige luxury templates that rely on empty slogans, identical rounded cards, and oversized serif headlines.
- Copying LABONI's existing layout. LABONI is a factual and product-data source, not a visual template.
- Checkout or admin interactions that trade familiarity and clarity for decorative motion.
- Invented specifications, shipping fees, tax rates, legal approval, stock, reviews, or payment success.

## Design Principles

1. Let verified products lead. Photography, real material information, and exact SKUs carry more trust than promotional claims.
2. Make configuration unmistakable. The chosen size, colour, fabric, mattress, price, stock, and SKU stay visibly connected.
3. Separate confidence from decoration. Brand surfaces can feel expressive; checkout, account, and admin remain calm and familiar.
4. Treat unknown business rules as configuration gaps, never content opportunities.
5. Preserve the customer's language and context from discovery through confirmation and email.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All primary flows support keyboard navigation, visible focus, labelled controls, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, screen-reader status updates, 44px mobile targets, and error messages that identify both the problem and recovery action. German and English content remain complete across storefront, cart, checkout, account, errors, and legal navigation.
